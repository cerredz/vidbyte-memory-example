# Domain facts

### F-001: price row source and proof link
This applies to every row of the price table that is written or rechecked, and to both its input and its output price. Each price is the one the row's provider charges for that model and tier on its own API, and the source_url column holds the address of that provider's own pricing or documentation page where the price is shown. The owner asked that the pricing correspond to what the providers serve on their own APIs, with documentation links inserted as proof for each price. Before writing a row, open the provider's own page, take both prices for the exact model and tier from it, and put that page's address in source_url so the price can be confirmed from the link alone. A price found only on another company's page, such as an aggregator, reseller or comparison site, or recalled from memory, does not meet this rule even when the number looks right.

### F-002: regular-rate price row and proof link
This applies to every row of the price table that is written or rechecked, and to both its input and its output price. Each price is the regular rate the row's provider charges for that model and tier on its own API, never a promotional or sale rate, and the source_url column holds the address of that provider's own pricing or documentation page where the price is shown. The owner asked that the pricing correspond to what the providers serve on their own APIs, with documentation links inserted as proof for each price, and then asked that each price be integrated at its regular price rather than at a promotion or sale the provider was running. Before writing a row, open the provider's own page, take both regular prices for the exact model and tier from it, and put that page's address in source_url; a promotional or sale rate shown there may be recorded only in the note column, never in a price column or as a row or tier of its own. A price found only on another company's page, such as an aggregator or reseller, or recalled from memory, does not meet this rule even when the number looks right, and a discount the provider calls permanent was not decided by the owner, so this fact does not settle it.
