Format: `<term>: <meaning in one or two sentences>`, then the metadata line. See `memory/MAP.md`.

### T-001: regular price
regular price: The rate a provider lists for API use of a model and tier when no promotion or sale applies, which is the rate the owner asked every price row to carry on 2026-10-11 (FB-002). A promotional or sale rate is not the regular price even while it is the rate being billed; whether a discount the provider calls permanent, or a time-of-day rate, counts as regular is open (Q-001, Q-003).
